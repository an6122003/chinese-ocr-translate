HanLens — Chinese on screen → pinyin, Hán Việt, Vietnamese

1. Drag HanLens onto the Applications folder in this window.
2. Open Applications and double-click HanLens.
   macOS says it can't check HanLens for malware (it isn't from the App Store). Click Done, then open
   System Settings › Privacy & Security, scroll down, and click "Open Anyway" next to HanLens. Confirm with
   your password or Touch ID. You only do this once.
3. The welcome window asks for two permissions — Screen Recording and Accessibility. Grant both, then click
   Relaunch.

Use it: press Control + Option + S, then drag a box or draw a circle around any Chinese text.
Needs macOS 26 (Tahoe) or later.
